# Green-light criteria (agreed with Kinjal 2026-10-28, before results)
1. Early warning on most cascade runs, median lead >= 60 s (M1).
2. M1 warns earlier than the end-to-end monitor (M6) at a similar false-alarm rate.
3. M1 beats or matches M2, M3, M5, and the paper says where and why.
4. M1 false alarms < 1 per hour on held-out control time.
5. Alibaba: the August numbers reproduce, and the forward evaluation shows signal (naive CRS AUC > 0.5, with CI).
6. One clear, transferable finding (e.g. which fault types give warning, and why).
7. 10 pages ACM, anonymized, every number traced to a results file, repo complete.
All 7 = green; 1-4 and 7 = yellow; 1 or 2 fail = red.
