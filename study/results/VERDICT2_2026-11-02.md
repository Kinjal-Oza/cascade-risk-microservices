**Joint reading with campaign 1 (276 scored runs, 2 apps):**
- Gradual faults do give early warning, a median 60–90 s before the SLO breach.
- That warning comes from an adaptive baseline on latency. Watching the user-facing latency itself is as early as, or earlier than, per-service, call-graph or throttling signals.
- A static threshold "leads" only by alarming constantly.
- The CRS adds no timing benefit. In campaign 1 it had the fewest false alarms; in campaign 2 it had more than latency alone.
